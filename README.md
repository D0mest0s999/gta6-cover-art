Vice Grid Cover Studio turns your photos into GTA-style multi-panel poster covers: 9 layouts (Hero + 3, Nine Grid, Full Bleed, Split Duo, Tilted Snaps, Quad, Magazine, Vertical Strips, Centre Hero), 6 sunset/neon colour grades, per-panel crop and positioning (drag to move, scroll to zoom, or use the sliders), editable title + script subtitle in 4 lettering styles (Bold Block, Halftone Pop, Neon Glow, Outline), film grain, vignette, poster frame, badge ribbon and a heat-meter star row — then exports all 3 post sizes (1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1920×1080) as PNGs at once. Fully offline: your photos never leave your machine. Sample photos are AI-generated fictional people; the design is original — no Rockstar logo, artwork or fonts. Lettering uses Anton and Pacifico under the SIL Open Font License (files under `app/fonts/`).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — sample photos are already loaded, so it is three steps from open to PNG: pick a layout, edit the caption, hit "Export ALL 3 sizes at once".
